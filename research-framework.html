Research-to-Artifact System

A repeatable process for turning ambiguous questions into decision-ready evidence.

Reusable framework, implemented and refined across multiple research efforts. A methodical research approach for business problems and personal questions alike, built to surface curated evidence from credible sources.
View the operating model Representative studies
At a glance

A 10-stage research lifecycle, from problem definition through quality audit to final delivery. Skipping a stage requires documenting why.

A 7-level decision authority hierarchy resolves every judgment call, from explicit instructions down to a bounded, documented assumption.

The convergence loop that produced this page ran 13 rounds before a confirmation audit found nothing left to fix.

The problem

What ordinary LLM research gets wrong

Jumps from a vague question straight to a polished answer, skipping evidence collection entirely.
Treats its own training data as a source, instead of something that needs a citation.
Confuses a popular opinion with a validated finding, or one good example with proof it generalizes.
Turns a recommendation into a hard requirement without flagging that a judgment call happened.
Delivers a well-written report with no way to trace a claim back to where it actually came from.
Mistakes a completed draft, or a single finished pass, for a completed research process.
Operating model

The process

Method selection

Methodology matched to context

Artifact-producing problem

Design Science Research Methodology governs by default, moving through problem definition, design, demonstration, evaluation, and communication.

Evidence-mapping-only question

A scoping/evidence-synthesis discipline governs instead, without claiming formal systematic-review status unless every requirement is actually met.

Causal question

Neither method applies alone. A causal, experimental, or systematic-review approach is required, and the process says so instead of forcing a fit.

Method turns out to be wrong mid-stream

The method gets replaced. A mismatched method doesn't get carried to the finish line for the sake of momentum.

Evidence & claim discipline

Every source is graded, every claim is classified

A

Primary, authoritative, or peer-reviewed evidence.

B

Strong external/institutional evidence or employer-syndicated primary content.

C

Practitioner guidance or a structured public artifact.

D

Discovery only, raising questions without establishing conclusions.

Source-supported fact Observed finding Estimate Inference Design requirement Design hypothesis Unknown

Four collapses this process is built to prevent:

  • Observation collapsing into causation.
  • Popularity collapsing into validation.
  • Example quality collapsing into effectiveness.
  • A recommendation collapsing into a requirement.
AI & human boundaries

Where the model stops and I start

AI supports

  • Discovery and query generation
  • Extraction and coding suggestions
  • Synthesis across sources
  • Contradiction checks
  • Drafting
  • QA and visual planning

AI may not

  • Treat its own memory as evidence
  • Fabricate support for a claim
  • Silently change methodology mid-stream
  • Hide uncertainty to sound more confident
  • Infer an outcome the data doesn't support
  • Convert my preference into a research fact
  • Make the final approval decision

Human authority retains

  • Defining and approving the decision problem
  • Approving consequential assumptions or changes
  • Owning how the final artifact gets used
  • Auditing for correctness
  • Determining human readability and usability
  • Redirecting further passes based on misdirected outputs or new research questions
  • Final decision authority
Decision authority hierarchy
  1. Explicit instructions
  2. Controlling project authorities
  3. Locked objectives and constraints
  4. Strongest applicable evidence
  5. Reasoned inference
  6. Bounded, documented assumption
  7. Escalation, if a blocker exists
What counts as a blocker

A blocker exists only when a decision materially affects validity or usefulness, the available evidence can't resolve it, more than one defensible path remains, and proceeding on an assumption would compromise the research. Everything short of that is resolved through the hierarchy above.

Disconfirmation & convergence

Execute, find the fault, repair, repeat

A convergence loop runs the same review pass repeatedly, finding and repairing faults or gaps until the delta between passes reaches zero. This page is itself a product of that loop. The same research framework it describes governed how this page's own content and structure took shape.

Convergence record excerpt

Four of the thirteen convergence rounds behind this page.

Round 1
Fault: "Non-code" only described an absence. It named no actual capability. Repair: reframed around Workflow, Decision, and Research/Intelligence system jobs. Delta: high.
Round 4
Fault: a planning case study risked reading as career-advice documentation. Repair: centered the decision architecture, controls, authority, and execution transition instead. Delta: high.
Round 9
Fault: a minimalist homepage risked hiding credibility. Repair: adopted layered progressive disclosure with visible status/evidence cues. Delta: moderate.
Round 13
Confirmation audit. No new substantive structural fault found. Delta: zero. Convergence reached.

Portfolio Architecture Research

Research complete

Already governing this site's structure.

Researched and designed the evidence architecture this portfolio itself runs on.

18portfolios sampled
3system taxonomy
Question

How should an Applied AI portfolio present a workflow system, a decision system, and a research system as one coherent identity without overstating what any of them actually prove?

Method

Design Science Research Methodology combined with structured evidence mapping, current Applied AI role-market analysis, and a purposive sample of 18 public portfolios.

Evidence streams

Hiring and work-sample research, ePortfolio studies, information-architecture research, current job postings from OpenAI, Anthropic, and AWS, and this project's own internal artifacts.

Contradiction & repair

The first framing split projects into code and non-code, describing what two of them lacked instead of what they actually demonstrated. The repair reorganized the taxonomy around the job each system performs: workflow, decision, or research.

Resulting artifact

The architecture guide, evidence register, and case-page structure this site actually uses.

What this enabled

Set the homepage layout, the shared case-page spine, and the evidence-status labeling convention used on every page here, and identified which functional areas to include based on industry best practices and other viable insights the research surfaced.

AI Value Realization in Website Design Agencies

Research-centered strategy case

No implementation or measured outcome yet.

Studied where AI creates or erodes economic value in website design agencies, then deepened the same research against one real agency's own numbers.

18sources
28evidence records
Question

Where does AI-driven efficiency in a website design agency actually go, and under what conditions can an agency keep it instead of passing it straight to clients?

Method

A mixed-evidence research pass with source-quality grading, claim-to-evidence mapping, and horizon modeling for the industry as a whole, then a second pass applying the same discipline to one real agency's own support-hour and project-margin data, with the company and its personnel anonymized.

Evidence streams

Direct agency surveys, client-side demand research, platform telemetry, labor-market data, and technical capability benchmarks, plus one real operator's own support and project data for the deepening.

Contradiction & repair

The default assumption was that AI-driven time savings should raise agency margin. The industry-level research found productivity gains widely coexisting with weak monetization, and the agency-specific deepening showed why: an hourly support model passes freed-up time straight to the client as extra work instead of turning it into agency margin.

Resulting artifact

A 28-page executive research report with an evidence backbone and a 2029 horizon model, deepened into an operator-specific value-flow model with commercialization gates.

What this enabled

Gave the operator a five-year strategic positioning basis specific to its own numbers and constraints. It does not replace a mission-specific analysis, and it does not select which course of action the company should take. The analysis also doesn't have the company's complete internal data, ongoing follow-on research, or a maintained database behind it, which limits how far the five-year positioning can be trusted without further work.

Enterprise Agent Governance

Research package complete

Implementation not yet demonstrated.

Researched how enterprises should govern AI agent authority across every channel an agent can actually act through, beyond any single protocol.

35source evidence backbone
15claim confidence map
Question

How should enterprises govern AI agent authority across MCP, CLI execution, Skills, and direct generated code before scaling agent use?

Method

A six-step evidence-backed architecture synthesis built specifically to test its own starting assumption, using a 35-source evidence backbone and a 15-claim confidence map.

Evidence streams

Standards and open-project sources, primary vendor technical documentation, empirical research, and security guidance.

Contradiction & repair

The project began from the assumption that MCP was becoming the primary governance surface. The evidence showed agents routinely acting through channels that bypass MCP entirely, so the premise was narrowed to the actual channel-agnostic authority surface.

Resulting artifact

A protocol-agnostic agent control-plane reference architecture and a security-signoff model.

What this enabled

Identifies the highest-leverage governance opportunities in applied AI, the kind that could guide project selection and cross-domain functional integration if this were applied at enterprise scale.

Outcome & limits

What this proves, and what it doesn't yet

This research framework compiles findings through a transparent method, built specifically to inform strategy development and planning. It runs through intake, methodology gate, evidence discipline, convergence, and human review, and it has now been refined across the three studies above.

The framework is inherently limited by the nature of open-source information, and it can be further refined through individual use once hard data becomes available and connected. I'm also refining small details in the final PDF format, slowly minimizing the routine edits that can be defined before the process even begins. Any research here should be read as signal. Actual business decisions or assumptions still need multiple sources behind them before they're considered confident.
